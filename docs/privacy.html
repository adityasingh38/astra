Privacy policy.

ASTRA uses the information you provide to calculate and save your birth chart and show written readings. You can export your data, remove a birth profile, or delete your account.

Information you provide

When you create an account, ASTRA uses your email address and password for sign-in. Your saved birth profiles can include a preferred name, birth date and time, birthplace, coordinates, time zone, and chart settings. ASTRA stores calculated chart snapshots linked to your profiles. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; ASTRA does not receive them in its chart API.

How ASTRA uses it

ASTRA sends your birth details to its calculation API to compute Western and Vedic chart facts, relationship comparisons, transits, and Moolank. Readings are assembled from written passages stored in ASTRA. The current app does not send your birth details to a generative AI service. Birthplace searches send the place words you type to a server using OpenStreetMap Nominatim data. ASTRA does not use birthplace autocomplete.

Service providers

Supabase hosts authentication and saved account data. Render hosts the chart API. OpenStreetMap Nominatim provides birthplace search data. These providers process the data needed for those functions. ASTRA does not sell your personal data or include advertising trackers in the current app.

Keeping and deleting data

Saved birth profiles and chart snapshots remain associated with your account until you delete them or the account. In the app, open You to export your data, remove a profile, or delete your account. You can also delete your account on this website. Deleting an account removes the authentication record and its linked birth profiles and chart snapshots from the active database. Service provider backup retention may continue for a limited period under their policies.

Security and choices

ASTRA uses authenticated requests and database row access rules so users can access their own saved profiles. You may use manual coordinates and time zone entry instead of birthplace search. You can request help or ask a privacy question at hello@trysurge.in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will appear here.
